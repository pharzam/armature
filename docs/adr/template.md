# NNNN. Short noun phrase describing the decision

Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Status

## Context

The forces at play, stated in a value-neutral way: what problem pushed this
decision, what constraints applied, what evidence was on hand. Link the deep-dive
that holds the detail — the ADR records the decision, not the research.

## Decision

The response to those forces, in active voice: "We will …". One decision per ADR.
Say what was chosen *and* what was rejected, so a later reader does not re-open a
closed question.

## Consequences

The resulting context, positive and negative. What becomes easier, what becomes
harder, what new work this creates, and what stays open (numbers left unfrozen,
follow-up ADRs expected).
